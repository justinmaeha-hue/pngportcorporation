About this portal

A modern starting point for general information about maritime activity in Papua New Guinea.

Maritime transport is important to a country made up of islands, coastal communities, and geographically separated provinces. Ports help connect people, goods, businesses, and regional markets.

This independent concept brings general maritime information into one place. It does not claim to represent a government department, state-owned enterprise, bank, or investment provider.

  • General maritime and port information
  • Informational notices and updates
  • Service-directory layout
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • A dashboard interface for design testing
Our design principles

Clarity. Security. Accountability.

Public information should be easy to understand. Users should be able to identify who operates a website, which services are genuine, and whether a transaction is real or only a demonstration.

This template does not collect passwords, banking details, identity documents, or withdrawal PINs.
